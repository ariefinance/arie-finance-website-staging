About ARIE Finance

Who we are

Operating from Mauritius, ARIE Finance is a boutique international payment platform specializing in cross-border transactions, with a particular focus on traditionally underserved and rapidly-growing markets.

We are an FSC-licensed payment intermediary, serving internationally active businesses with cross-border payment infrastructure and relationship-led service.

Under the expert guidance of financial industry veterans both in Mauritius and the UK, the company offers a broad range of global payment and financial advisory services to cater to the evolving needs of small and medium-sized international businesses.
