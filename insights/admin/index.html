Article examples & guidance

You can create an Insight in two ways:

  1. Write an original article for an external business audience.
  2. Adapt suitable material from the internal ARIE Digest for a public audience — rewrite it in your own words; do not copy it.

There is no automatic Digest → Insights import. Every article still needs ARIE’s internal review before it is published.

Example — ARIE Digest, 7 October 2026:

  • “Africa Credit Rating Agency launches from Mauritius” could become a Guides or Updates article on what a Mauritius-based rating agency means for internationally active businesses.
  • “SAP Pay” could become an Updates article on a relevant international-payments development.

Suitable public themes: Mauritius financial-services developments, international-payment developments, business-relevant regulatory developments, practical payment Guides, and ARIE company news.

Internal only — do NOT adapt for the public blog: Film & TV intelligence; Oil & Gas intelligence; ARIE relevance / management commentary; internal QA, research-control or deduplication notes; internal source-access limitations; management-only conclusions.
